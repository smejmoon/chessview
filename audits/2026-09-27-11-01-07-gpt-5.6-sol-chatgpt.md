### Renderer wiring is still outside the Recenter contract tests

**Evidence:** `docs/components/interface.md §Verification` requires contract coverage for “Recenter to a known target by mini-board click” and “Recenter after a legal Nodus Move.” The live mapping from rendered UI to those commands remains in `src/nodus-renderer.js`: `bindControls()` binds rendered `[data-nav-key]` buttons through `bindRecenterTarget`, `renderSatellites()` separately binds generated mini-board wrappers through the same helper, and the center Chessground config assigns `createBoardMoveRecenterHandler(...)` to `movable.events.after`. The new `test/recenter-input.test.js` tests `bindRecenterTarget` and `createBoardMoveRecenterHandler` directly with a fake element and direct handler calls, but never constructs `NodusRenderer` or observes its Chessground configuration. The complete current repository tree has no renderer/browser contract test, and `package.json` defines a Node-only `node --test` harness with no DOM/browser test dependency. Removing one of those renderer call sites, or failing to attach the Chessground `after` callback, would therefore leave the new helper tests green while breaking the required user interaction.

**Why it matters:** the architectural simplification makes presentation-to-Recenter mapping a deliberate application boundary, but regression protection currently starts one layer below the live integration. A mini-board or played-board move can stop issuing Recenter commands even while controller, materialization, promotion, and helper tests all continue to pass.

**Suggested next action:** add the smallest renderer-level contract test that exercises the real `NodusRenderer` composition for at least one rendered target click and the center-board move callback. Keep the existing helper tests; the missing coverage is specifically that the renderer installs those helpers correctly. Prefer a narrow injectable Chessground/DOM seam or minimal fixture rather than introducing a general browser abstraction solely for testing.
