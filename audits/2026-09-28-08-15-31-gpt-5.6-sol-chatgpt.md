## Findings

### Exact-tip verification has two maintained owners

**Evidence:** `docs/components/delivery.md` §Verification defines the exact-tip evidence procedure as four maintained steps: resolve the task branch to SHA `X`, find the `CI` run with `head_sha == X`, require the latest-attempt `Verify` job to conclude `success`, then re-resolve the branch before accepting that evidence. `chatgpt/branch-preview.md` §Implement independently repeats the same four-step algorithm, and §Ready to merge restates the same acceptance rule again. Both files are changed in this change set. Delivery's stated purpose includes automated verification, while branch-preview explicitly says it owns the feature-development lifecycle and command meanings.

**Why it matters:** A future change to what counts as valid exact-tip evidence now requires semantically synchronized edits in two durable owners. If only one copy changes, branch preflight can apply a different merge-readiness gate from the Delivery verification contract even though both documents present their wording as current procedure.

**Suggested next action:** Keep the exact-tip evidence algorithm in `docs/components/delivery.md` §Verification as the single maintained owner. In `chatgpt/branch-preview.md`, keep only the lifecycle-specific obligations to establish/re-establish exact-tip verification after implementation, distillation, and during ready-to-merge preflight, and point those obligations to `docs/components/delivery.md` §Verification instead of duplicating the algorithm. Right Place disposition: `finding: keep`; `boundary: confirmed` — Delivery is the concrete owner of verification semantics, while branch-preview consumes that contract as part of its lifecycle.
